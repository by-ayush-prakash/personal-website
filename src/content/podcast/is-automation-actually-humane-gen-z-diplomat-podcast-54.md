<p>Is automation really humane, or is this a misinterpretation? Automation is always thought of as a humane endeavour. Ridding humans of dull, boring, dangerous, and meaningless jobs has always been the goal. But is automation actually humane? From my diagnosis, no, its not. The mere administration of automation is inhumane, and many factors support this argument.
