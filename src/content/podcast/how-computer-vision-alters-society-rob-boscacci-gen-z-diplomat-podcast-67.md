<p>Robert Boscacci is the author of “The Spring of Sight: An Inflection Point for Computer Vision and Society,” a book about how our society is changing, adapting, and being toppled over by computer vision technologies. This episode will teach you what computer vision is and how it differs from AI, how computer vision factors into automation, how the healthcare industry changes with computer vision, the most difficult part about writing about technology’s effects on society, if we should regulate computer vision, how Gen Z should deal with the effects of computer vision on society, and lots more in the 67th episode of the Gen Z Diplomat podcast.</p>
