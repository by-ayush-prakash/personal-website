<p>Sean League is the Co-Founder and VP of spacecraft development at SpaceFab. His mission is to enable long term human presence in space, with a focus to create a space manufacturing service that allows everyone, from governments to individuals, to affordably build their own space projects. &nbsp;</p><p>Space mining has been the goal of many companies for many decades. Yet, barely any progress has been made. Introduce SpaceFab, with a vision which blows all competition out of the water. They are interested in contributing to the genesis of the space economy, and in fact, are already doing so. &nbsp;</p><p>This episode will teach you how the 60s space race pales in comparison to the one occurring in the 21st century, what the space economy will look like in the near future, how COVID impacted the space mining industry, why SpaceX is a game changer for the space industry, whether we will see alien civilizations sooner or later, how AI and robotics are used in space, and more in the 51st episode of the Gen Z Diplomat podcast. &nbsp;</p>
