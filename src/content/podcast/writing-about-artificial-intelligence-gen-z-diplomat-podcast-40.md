<p>This episode is dedicated to journey about writing my first book, AI for Gen Z, Data to Stop Disruption. </p><p>With so much happening in the world of AI, headlines can make it seem like things are steady or uneventful for certain periods of time. This is not the case, and in fact, I have found that certain news reported about artificial intelligence only discusses very marginal things, like art creation or robotic advances. This is not what my book is about. </p><p>My book is about the future of AI for Gen Z. Autonomous weapons, virtual politicians, and job automation make up only some of the many problems that AI imposes on my generation. How do you explain the complex intricacies of this technology to a generation with the attention span of a Tweet? Hard work, discipline, focus, and lots of caffeine. </p>
