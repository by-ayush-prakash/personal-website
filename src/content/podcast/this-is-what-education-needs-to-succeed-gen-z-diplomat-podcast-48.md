<p>In the 21st century, "modern" schooling systems are outdated, stagnant, and don't take advantage of the latest science and technology. As such, we are setting contemporary students up for failure, as our future requires people with dynamic skillsets that are unfound in current classrooms. </p><p>Education 2.0 is the most important societal overhaul we will ever undertake. Simply put, without this reconfiguration, we will descend, slowly then quickly, into ruin because our new generations are being taught with subpar methods. Students' health through sleep is uncared for, we still use a silly grading system to pass or fail, there is no creativity present, and worst, the most powerful algorithms are not being used to personalize curriculums to get the most out of students. </p><p>Expect to learn how the sleeping patterns school forces on students is literally killing them, some examples of new grading techniques that can enhance learning, how to incorporate creativity into learning, why personalized algorithms could be the revolution in education we need, the drawbacks associated with this transformation, and much more... </p>
