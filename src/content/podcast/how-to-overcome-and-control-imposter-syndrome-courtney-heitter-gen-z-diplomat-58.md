<p>Courtney Heitter is the host of the “You’re Not Qualified” podcast, a motivational podcast which aims to overcome any un-qualifications for any type of goal. Imposter syndrome is something that a vast majority of people suffer from whether they know about it or not. Courtney is making the world a better place by telling you to pick your actions and don&#39;t let your qualifications, or lack thereof, stop you from making a difference in the ways you want to. </p><p><br></p><p>This episode will teach you what the purpose of the You’re Not Qualified podcast is, what Courtney’s background with imposter syndrome and not being qualified is, if Gen Z are in the perfect storm when it comes to imposter syndrome, how Gen Z can overcome or control imposter syndrome, how whales can help with climate change, advice for those dealing with imposter syndrome, and more in the 58th episode of the Gen Z Diplomat podcast. </p>
