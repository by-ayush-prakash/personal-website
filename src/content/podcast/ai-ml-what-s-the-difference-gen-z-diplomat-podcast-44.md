<p>This episode is dedicated to understanding the difference between artificial intelligence and machine learning. &nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>In the real world, most of what we talk about as AI is really ML. Social media algorithms, Boston Dynamics’ robots, and Google Assistant, to name a few, are simply machine learning tools designed to improve your experience and themselves. &nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>However, notifying this difference is important for the progression and inevitable emergence of human-like intelligence cased in silicon. We are not working towards statistics, we are working towards an unprecedented leap in our pursuit of increasingly exponential technologies. God-like powers have been bestowed unto us, and we must wield them with the wisdom of Gods as well — at least, our closest approximation.</p>
