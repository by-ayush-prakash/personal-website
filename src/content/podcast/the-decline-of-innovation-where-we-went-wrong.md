<p>If you feel that things are stagnant, here's a breakdown of why that is the case. </p><p>Innovation has always been thought of as assuredly exponential. But in recent decades, things have changed. Markets have started to respond to incremental improvements rather than risky ideas, and thus, companies and startups are gravitating towards the same thing, again and again. With that, we are reaching a plateau in scientific breakthroughs, furthering our detriment of radical progress and civilizational advancement. </p><p>This episode will teach you what innovation is, some examples of how and where it has declined, how agricultural technology plays a massive role in determining if innovation is declining, the concerns with a decline in progress, the importance of markets, and lots more in the 52nd episode of the Gen Z Diplomat podcast. </p>
