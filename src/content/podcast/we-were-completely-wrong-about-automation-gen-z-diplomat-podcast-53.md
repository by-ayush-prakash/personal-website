<p>The way we’ve all been talking about automation is totally incorrect. But why?</p><p>Automation has been the supposed seismic shift in the economy. We were told by economists and futurists alike that AI, robots, and other technologies would replace low-skill, low-wage workers, forcing them on a path of meaninglessness and universal basic income survival. However, this notion is very narrow.</p><p>This episode will teach you why automation is not this massive workforce shift, a brief overview of why innovation has been slowing down, why I started on the path of teaching Gen Z about automation, and lots more in the 53rd edition of the Gen Z Diplomat podcast.</p><p>#automation #artificialintelligence #innovation</p>
