He also founded Immigrant Wealth, teaching first-generation immigrants how to build wealth in Canada. &nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Canada is the best place for first-generation immigrants. From diversity to opportunity, the incentives for moving to Canada speak for themselves. Max saw this from the beginning, and built the largest online community centred around making the move to Canada for anyone as easy as possible.
