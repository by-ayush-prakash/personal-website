<p>This episode is dedicated to my thoughts about the current state of modern education. As it stands, education institutions must be rebuilt from the ground up in order to best serve future generations. We cannot continue the absurdity of factory education for further decades, it is destroying the potential of our youth. &nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>School should be an expansive and diverse playground of ideas and personalities. Students should be free to learn in the style that suits them, not the style that suits the school. The term factory education is appropriate, because everyone that comes out of school nowadays is indoctrinated with cookie-cutter information. &nbsp;School, in its current form, is lacking in many areas. However, the concept and the goals it seeks are powerful and necessary to keep our society and overall civilization afloat and progressing strongly. &nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>I just think that we have reached a plateau, a lack of innovation for education. We need more leaders, professors, teachers, and students to work together to construct the next generation of education.</p>
