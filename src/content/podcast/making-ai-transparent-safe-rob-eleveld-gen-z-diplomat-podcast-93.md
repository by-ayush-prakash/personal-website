<p>Rob Eleveld is the Chairman of Transparency Coalition. Due to concerns around the speed and scale of Generative AI rollout by BigTech, he has co-founded Transparency Coalition.ai, a 501c4 non-profit that advocates for transparency around the usage of “training data,” the products of human imagination, identity, and innovation (produced by creators and everyday users). Training data is the very foundation for which Generative AI systems are built, and much of it is personal data, copyrighted material, and unverified content. Currently completely unregulated, Generative AI is being built on digital data at massive scale without permissions, licenses, or quality assurance. All one needs to look at is a the social media disaster for our kids and teens to know that something needs to be done. As former FCC Chairman Tom Wheeler says in a recent book &quot;Techlash: Who Makes The Rules In The Digital Gilded Age&quot;: &quot;AI technology is being released with fewer safety standards and less oversight than that required for a toaster…. The lesson of the digital era thus far is a message about what happens when policy makers fail to get in front of the new technology’s effects. That story cannot be allowed to repeat in a world driven by artificial intelligence.” https://www.transparencycoalition.ai/ #ai #regulation #legal -<br>
