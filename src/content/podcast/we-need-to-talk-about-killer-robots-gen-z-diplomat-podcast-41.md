<p>This episode is dedicated to talking about the existential state of killer robots in our world, and how our ethics are being dissolved for the sake of warfare, money, and power. &nbsp;</p><p>Killer robots have always been feared by scientists and researchers who understand (and give their inputs on) the bleeding-edge of technology. This is why since 2015 there have been firm stances by AI and robotics folks against the proliferation of autonomous weapons. Stances which were in vain. &nbsp;</p><p>Each year, the development of weapons which do not require humans to act and/or react is accelerated and pumped with money and zero hesitancy. Simply because, according to game theory, we must do this before anyone else can, otherwise we automatically lose. These repercussions, however, make themselves known off of the battlefield that killer robots are designed for. Police departments could be equipped in the very near future with autonomous weapons of mass destruction. What happens next is anyone's guess. &nbsp;</p>
