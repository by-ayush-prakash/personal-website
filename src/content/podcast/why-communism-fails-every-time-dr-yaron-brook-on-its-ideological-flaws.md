<p>This is episode 102 of the Gen Z Diplomat Podcast.</p><p><br /></p><p>Yaron Brook is a objectivist philosopher, podcaster, and author. Dr. Brook authors works that inspire critical thinking. His most recent book, “In Pursuit of Wealth: The Moral Case for Finance,” makes the case that few industries are more vital to our prosperity – and more maligned – than the financial industry. Brook and coauthor Don Watkins explain why finance has faced so much criticism, and why, despite the conventional image of financiers as “greedy” and reckless, finance is a moral industry. Brook and Watkins also authored national best seller “Free Market Revolution: How Ayn Rand’s Ideas Can End Big Government” and “Equal Is Unfair: America’s Misguided Fight Against Income Inequality.”</p><p><br /></p><p>Connect with Yaron: </p><p> https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCabMx-URCjr2toe9wOE3Y-Q</p>
